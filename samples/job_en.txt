Junior Data Analyst — Anatolia Retail Analytics (fictional company), Istanbul (hybrid)

About us
Anatolia Retail Analytics helps mid-sized retail chains in Türkiye make better decisions with data. Our analytics team of 8 works with store, sales and inventory data from more than 300 stores.

What you will do
- Build and maintain recurring sales and inventory reports and dashboards for our retail clients.
- Write SQL queries to extract and combine data from our PostgreSQL data warehouse.
- Clean, validate and analyse large datasets using Python or Excel.
- Present findings and recommendations to non-technical stakeholders.
- Support senior analysts in ad-hoc analyses such as promotion performance and demand forecasting.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Industrial Engineering, Statistics, Economics, Computer Science or a related field (new graduates welcome).
- Solid SQL skills (joins, aggregations, window functions are a plus).
- Experience with Python (pandas) or R for data analysis.
- Experience with a BI tool such as Power BI or Tableau.
- Advanced Excel.
- Strong communication skills and ability to explain data to non-technical people.
- Professional working proficiency in English.

Nice to have
- Experience with retail, FMCG or supply chain data.
- Basic knowledge of statistics and forecasting methods.
- Familiarity with Git.
- Google Data Analytics or similar certificate.

We offer a structured mentoring program for new graduates, hybrid working and private health insurance.
